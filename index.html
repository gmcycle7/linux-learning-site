Learn Linux from Scratch

Welcome! This site is a complete, beginner-friendly guide to learning Linux. Whether you have never opened a terminal before or you know a few commands and want to get better, this guide will walk you through everything step by step.

How to use this guide

Start from Chapter 1 and work your way through in order. Each chapter builds on the previous one. Use the sidebar to jump to any topic. Use the search bar to find specific commands.

Learning Path

The chapters are arranged in the order a beginner should learn them:

Chapter 1
Linux Fundamentals
What Linux is, distributions, kernel vs shell, the filesystem, paths, environment variables, and users.
Chapter 2
Terminal Basics
Your first commands: pwd, ls, cd, man, echo, history, and getting around the terminal.
Chapter 3
Files & Directories
Create, copy, move, rename, delete files and folders. Links, file info, and the directory tree.
Chapter 4
Viewing & Editing Files
Read files with cat, less, head, tail. Edit with nano and vim. Transform text with sort, cut, tr, and more.
Chapter 5
Search & Filtering
Find files with find and locate. Search text with grep. Process text with awk and sed. Regex basics.
Chapter 6
Permissions & Ownership
Understand rwx, chmod, chown, sudo, umask. Keep your files safe and your system secure.
Chapter 7
Processes & Jobs
See what is running, stop processes, background jobs, priorities, and keeping programs alive.
Chapter 8
System & Disk Info
Check disk space, memory, CPU, uptime, block devices, and system identity.
Chapter 9
Networking Basics
IP addresses, ping, curl, wget, SSH, SCP, rsync, DNS tools, and basic network troubleshooting.
Chapter 10
Archives & Compression
Create and extract archives with tar, gzip, zip, and xz. Understand compression formats.
Chapter 11
Package Management
Install, update, and remove software. apt, dpkg, yum, dnf, rpm, and pacman across distributions.
Chapter 12
Shell & Scripting
Bash basics: pipes, redirection, variables, aliases, command substitution, and writing your first scripts.

Practical Skills & Reference

Chapter 13
Real-World Workflows
Practical recipes: find large files, search logs, batch rename, backup folders, and more.
Chapter 14
Troubleshooting & Safety
Fix common errors, avoid dangerous commands, recover from mistakes, and use man pages effectively.
Chapter 15
Command Comparisons
cp vs mv, grep vs find, apt vs apt-get, curl vs wget, soft vs hard links, and more side-by-side.

Advanced Topics

Chapter 16
Cron & Systemd
Schedule tasks with cron and crontab. Manage services and units with systemd, systemctl, and journalctl.
Chapter 17
Git Basics
Version control fundamentals: init, add, commit, branch, merge, push, pull, and working with remotes.
Chapter 18
Docker Basics
Container fundamentals: images, containers, Dockerfiles, volumes, networking, and docker-compose.
